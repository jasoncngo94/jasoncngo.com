About

I'm a strategy and business development leader, and the work I enjoy most is where analysis turns into something real.

Since 2018 I've led business development at SK8 Biotechnologies, a food-ingredients company that makes antimicrobials, including bacteriophages, for food processors and poultry producers. In that time I've taken a new food-safety product from launch to an established revenue line, built a greenfield manufacturing plant in the Philippines, and advised the leadership team through the sale of a global food-ingredients business to private equity.

I started my career in finance, in wealth management and credit strategy, and I'm a CFA charterholder. I'm now completing my MBA at Ivey Business School.

Outside of work you'll find me curling, golfing or out for a run.
